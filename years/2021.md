# 2021

[Back to the full index](../README.md)

2021 opened with a new American president cleaning up after a chaotic election, and the magazine's own year-ahead cover admitted that the year ahead was a gamble.

---

## "Morning after in America": January 23, 2021

**What the cover shows:** New president Joe Biden, in a suit, a face mask and his famous aviator sunglasses, walks towards the White House carrying a mop and a bucket. The White House walls are stained with giant orange splatters, the colour linked to Donald Trump. The headline plays on Ronald Reagan's old slogan "Morning in America": this morning comes with a hangover.

**What was happening:** Biden had just taken office on January 20, 2021, days after Trump supporters attacked the US Capitol. America faced a raging pandemic, a weak economy and deep political division.

**The point it made:** Biden had a giant clean-up job: beat the virus, repair the economy and calm a divided country. The magazine said things could soon look up, but the mess was real.

**Verdict: Partly true.** Vaccines rolled out fast in 2021 and the economy bounced back strongly. But the country stayed deeply divided, and in 2022 inflation hit its highest level in 40 years, which badly hurt Biden's popularity.

*Sources: [Anycorp supplement](https://www.anycorp.com/supplement), [Milled newsletter archive](https://milled.com/the-economist-fr/the-outlook-for-america-looks-grim-but-that-could-quickly-change-p3T2z8s-oZGxqOm-)*

## "The World in 2021": annual preview issue, published late 2020

**What the cover shows:** A casino slot machine. Its spinning reels carry symbols instead of fruit: a virus, a masked face, a vaccine syringe, Joe Biden's face, a split American flag, the Chinese flag, a dollar sign, a wind turbine, the TikTok logo and a burning tree. The lever of the machine is topped with a globe.

**What was happening:** The issue was made at the end of 2020, just as the first COVID vaccines were approved and Biden had won the US election. The whole world felt like a gamble.

**The point it made:** The magazine said 2021 would be decided by luck and by big open questions: vaccines against the virus, Biden against division, and action on climate change.

**Verdict: Partly true.** In 2021 vaccines did roll out across rich countries, but the virus kept mutating, with the Delta and Omicron waves. The split flag fitted too: the year began with the January 6 attack on the US Capitol.

*Sources: [Schweizer Standpunkt](https://www.schweizer-standpunkt.ch/news-detailansicht-en-wirtschaft/2021-the-world-as-a-gambling-hell.html), [The Istanbul Chronicle](https://www.theistanbulchronicle.com/post/story-behind-the-economist-s-covers)*
