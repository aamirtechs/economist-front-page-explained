# 2017

[Back to the full index](../README.md)

In 2017 a young outsider took power in France while the world watched a shouting match between America and North Korea and feared the worst. One cover hoped, the other warned. Both are worth studying.

---

## "Europe's saviour?": June 17, 2017

**What the cover shows:** A photo illustration of new French president Emmanuel Macron, in a suit, walking calmly on top of the sea, like Jesus walking on water. Behind him, the legs and hands of drowning people stick out of the water. These are the other European leaders who are sinking.

**What was happening:** Macron, a 39 year old outsider, had just won the French election in May 2017, beating the far right. Britain was leaving the EU, Merkel looked weaker in Germany, and many people hoped Macron would save the European project and reform France.

**The point it made:** The question mark is the point. The magazine asked whether one young leader could really rescue Europe and fix France, and doubted that anyone could be a saviour.

**Verdict: Partly true.** Macron did become Europe's leading voice, pushed for a stronger EU, and won a second term in 2022. But he did not transform Europe, and at home he faced the huge Yellow Vest protests from late 2018 and stayed unpopular for long periods.

## "It could happen": August 5, 2017

**What the cover shows:** A huge nuclear mushroom cloud rises over a burnt, empty land. Inside the cloud you can see two faces in profile: Kim Jong Un of North Korea on one side and Donald Trump on the other. A small blue Twitter bird sits on a bare twig, watching.

**What was happening:** North Korea had just tested missiles that could reach America, and US intelligence said it could now fit a small nuclear bomb on a missile. Trump and Kim were trading threats, and days later Trump promised "fire and fury" if North Korea threatened America again.

**The point it made:** The magazine warned that the two sides could blunder into a nuclear war by mistake, even if neither side truly wanted one. Its cover story was called "How to avoid nuclear war with North Korea".

**Verdict: Did not happen.** There was no war. In 2018 and 2019 Trump and Kim met in person three times, the first meetings ever between American and North Korean leaders, and the threats calmed down. North Korea kept its nuclear weapons, but it stopped testing long range missiles for several years.
