# 2024

[Back to the full index](../README.md)

2024 was the biggest election year in history. The magazine's year-ahead cover called it correctly, and its October cover praised an American economy that really was beating the world.

---

## "The World Ahead 2024": annual preview issue, published November 2023

**What the cover shows:** In the middle sits a ballot box with voting envelopes, under an hourglass that shows time running out. Around it are illustrated profiles of leaders such as Putin and Zelensky, a falling money graph surrounded by the dollar, pound, euro and yuan with a question mark, plus wind turbines and icons for AI and space.

**What was happening:** 2024 was set to be the biggest election year in history, with votes in America, India, Europe and dozens of other places. Wars in Ukraine and Gaza were raging, and interest rates were at their highest in years.

**The point it made:** The magazine said democracy itself was on the ballot in 2024, and that the US election would be the biggest story of a dangerous year.

**Verdict: Came true.** More than 60 countries held elections in 2024, and Donald Trump won the US presidency in November, the result the whole world had been watching. The wars in Ukraine and Gaza continued through the year.

## "The envy of the world": October 19, 2024

**Cover line:** "The envy of the world", with the sub-line "America's economy: a special report".

**What the cover shows:** A 100 dollar bill launches into the sky like a rocket, with flames coming out beneath it. America's money is shown as a machine blasting upwards while other countries watch.

**What was happening:** After COVID, the American economy had grown much faster than Europe's or Japan's. American incomes, jobs and stock markets were all beating the rest of the rich world, even though many American voters felt unhappy about prices.

**The point it made:** The magazine argued that America's economy was the strongest in the rich world and the envy of everyone else, whoever won the coming election.

**Verdict: Came true.** For 2024 the claim held up: US growth beat every other big rich economy. The caveat came later: in 2025 Trump's tariffs and market swings made many people ask how long the success would last.
