One day defined 2001, and the magazine's answer was one of its simplest and most famous covers: a photograph, and five words.

---

## "The day the world changed": September 15, 2001

**What the cover shows:** A photograph of New York just after the attacks. A huge cloud of smoke and dust hides the Manhattan skyline where the Twin Towers of the World Trade Center had stood only days before.

**What was happening:** On September 11, 2001, al-Qaeda hijackers flew passenger planes into the World Trade Center and the Pentagon, killing nearly 3,000 people. It was the worst terrorist attack in history.

**The point it made:** This was not a forecast. The cover said that this one day had changed America, and with it the whole world, in the same way Pearl Harbor had in 1941.

**Verdict: Not a prediction.** It was a reaction to the news. The change it named did follow: America invaded Afghanistan in 2001 and Iraq in 2003, and airport and security rules changed all over the world.
