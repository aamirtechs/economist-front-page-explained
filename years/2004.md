# 2004

[Back to the full index](../README.md)

2004 was dominated by the Iraq war going wrong and an American election the magazine found depressing. It also spotted, early, the two big China stories of the next twenty years: pollution and health.

---

## "Resign, Rumsfeld": May 2004

**What the cover shows:** The famous "Hooded Man" photo from Abu Ghraib prison in Iraq. A prisoner stands on a box wearing a hood and a poncho, arms stretched out, with electric wires attached to his hands.

**What was happening:** Photos of American soldiers abusing Iraqi prisoners at Abu Ghraib prison were published in April and May 2004 and shocked the world. Donald Rumsfeld was America's defence secretary and in charge of the war in Iraq.

**The point it made:** The magazine said the abuse scandal was so bad, and the Iraq war was being run so poorly, that Rumsfeld had to resign.

**Verdict: Did not happen.** Rumsfeld kept his job for two and a half more years. He resigned only in November 2006, after his party lost the midterm elections and anger over the Iraq war had grown much wider.

*Source: [Newikis: Abu Ghraib](https://newikis.com/en/Abu_Ghraib_torture_and_prisoner_abuse)*

## "China's growing pains": August 21, 2004

**What the cover shows:** A Western style dragon, standing for China, wears a green suit and breathes out thick dirty smoke that covers the people behind it. Next to the dragon stands a doctor with his hands on his hips, looking angry but powerless.

**What was happening:** China's economy was growing at about 10 percent a year and changing world trade. At the same time its factories were polluting the air and water, the SARS disease had exposed weak public health, and the gap between rich cities and the poor countryside was growing.

**The point it made:** The cover said China's rise came with real pain at home, above all pollution and health problems, and that these problems could slow the country's rise if they were not fixed.

**Verdict: Partly true.** The pains did not stop China, which kept growing fast and became the world's second biggest economy in 2010. But the warning about pollution proved right: dirty air became a national crisis, and the government later had to spend heavily to clean it up.

*Source: [Xu, Painting Chinese Mythology (PDF)](https://xkunnet.github.io/publications/Xu_Painting%20Chinese%20Methology.pdf)*

## "The incompetent or the incoherent?": October 30, 2004

**What the cover shows:** Photos of the two candidates side by side. George W. Bush is labelled "The incompetent" and John Kerry is labelled "The incoherent". The leader carried the question as its title.

**What was happening:** America was days away from the 2004 presidential election. Bush was fighting for a second term while the Iraq war went badly, and Kerry was accused of changing his mind too often to be trusted.

**The point it made:** The magazine said both men were deeply flawed, but it backed Kerry "with a heavy heart", because it judged Bush too incompetent to deserve re-election, above all over Iraq.

**Verdict: Did not happen.** Their pick lost. Bush won the election on November 2, 2004, and stayed president until 2009.

*Sources: [LA Observed](http://www.laobserved.com/archive/2004/11/two_more_days.php), [Wikipedia: Editorial stance of The Economist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editorial_stance_of_The_Economist)*
