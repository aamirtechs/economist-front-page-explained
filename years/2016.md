# 2016

[Back to the full index](../README.md)

2016 was the year the old order cracked: Britain voted to leave the European Union and America elected Donald Trump. The two covers below are among the most shared the magazine has ever made.

---

## "Anarchy in the UK": July 2, 2016

**What the cover shows:** A flagpole with no proper flag on it. Instead, a pair of men's underpants printed with the British flag hangs from the pole like a sad flag, held together with safety pins, the symbol of punk rock. The title is taken from the Sex Pistols punk song. It makes Britain look broken and badly run.

**What was happening:** On June 23, 2016, British voters chose in a referendum to leave the European Union, called Brexit. Prime Minister David Cameron, who had wanted to stay, resigned at once. The pound fell hard and nobody knew who would lead the country or what came next.

**The point it made:** The magazine, which had supported staying in the EU, said Britain had voted for chaos and was sailing into a storm with nobody in control.

**Verdict: Partly true.** There was no real anarchy, and the state kept working. But the politics were chaotic for years: two more prime ministers, repeated failed votes in parliament, and long fights over the Brexit deal. Britain finally left the EU on January 31, 2020.

*Sources: [Studia Anglica Posnaniensia paper](https://reference-global.com/download/article/10.14746/stap.2023.58.07.pdf), [Biblioteka Nauki](https://bibliotekanauki.pl/articles/2031034.pdf)*

## "The new nationalism": November 19, 2016

**What the cover shows:** The artwork copies a famous American painting, "The Spirit of '76", which shows marching drummers from the American Revolution. Here the marchers are Donald Trump in the middle beating a drum, Vladimir Putin of Russia playing the fife, and Nigel Farage of Britain with a drum, while Marine Le Pen of France waves the French flag behind them. The cover story was titled "Trump's world".

**What was happening:** Donald Trump had just won the American election on a promise to "put America first". Britain had voted for Brexit, and nationalist leaders and parties were rising in Russia, France, Turkey, India and China at the same time.

**The point it made:** The magazine warned that, for the first time since the Second World War, the world's big powers were all turning to nationalism at once, and that this would make the world more dangerous.

**Verdict: Came true.** Trump brought in travel bans, trade tariffs and a trade war with China, and pulled America out of international deals. Nationalist governments won or held power in many countries, and "America First" style politics spread. The one name on the cover who did not win was Le Pen, who lost the French elections of 2017 and 2022.

*Sources: [The American Interest](https://www.the-american-interest.com/2016/12/07/the-nation-and-the-smells-of-home/), [Wikipedia: Neo-nationalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neo-nationalism)*
