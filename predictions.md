# The Predictions Board

[Back to the full index](README.md)

Every cover in this project, on one page. On the left, what the cover predicted or said. On the right, the status: did it happen, is it still pending, or is it a question for the future?

**The score so far, from 62 covers checked:** ✅ 21 happened · 🟡 25 partly happened · ❌ 6 did not happen · ⚪ 9 were not predictions · ⏳ 1 still pending

## How to read the status column

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| ✅ HAPPENED | Events matched what the cover said or warned |
| 🟡 PARTLY HAPPENED | Part of it happened, or it happened for a different reason |
| ❌ DID NOT HAPPEN | Events went the other way |
| ⚪ NOT A PREDICTION | A judgment, a portrait or a report, not a forecast |
| ⏳ PENDING | Too early to tell. The story belongs to the future |
